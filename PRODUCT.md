# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: student hackers deciding whether to apply. Also sponsors/partners and mentors/judges evaluating whether to back or join the event. 

## Product Purpose
Site for AKI HACKS 2026, a first-edition, India-based, tech-focused innovation hackathon (innovation is rewarded most, business viability second) in Kolkata, October 2026. Gets students to apply and sponsors/mentors to get in touch. Success: applications, sponsor/mentor inquiries. (The "Unlock Kolkata" vote and venue poll were dropped.)

## Positioning
First edition, India-based, Kolkata-rooted. Framed as building technology that hasn't been built yet: solve a real problem, ship a working prototype, innovation judged first. Brand motif 秋 (aki, autumn).

## Operating Context
Static single-page site (HTML, Tailwind CDN, Firebase Auth anonymous + Firestore). Collection: `support_messages` (contact + apply-interest). Next.js app at repo root (legacy static site in `docs/legacy/`). Pages: Home, Experience, Journey, Organisers, Sponsors, FAQ, Apply, Contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Date and venue are TBD.
- Undecided, must not be invented: prizes, tracks, schedule, sponsor list, whether Apply Now is full intake or interest capture.
- Existing page forbids shadows/glow (explicit no-glow CSS rule).
- Firebase config is client-side in the HTML.

## Brand Commitments
Logo assets: pixel-type "AKI HACKS" wordmark in black with orange 秋 (`WhatsApp Image 2026-09-28 at 8.35.53 PM.jpeg`). Orange `#F25D27`, ink `#070A0E`. Two other images (white D-shape, cyan "B" mark) are unidentified, likely partner/sponsor marks; confirm before use.

## Evidence on Hand
Organisers are real and named (confirmed by user; content lives in the page). No sponsors confirmed, no testimonials, no past-edition data (first edition). Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles
1. Be honest about what is undecided (TBD stays TBD).
2. One clear action for students: apply.
3. Sponsors and mentors get credible proof from real organisers, not invented logos.
4. Signals (counts, logos, names) are real, never faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Existing: skip link, focus-visible outline, reduced-motion support. Preserve.

- Teams: participants bring their own, 2-4 people.

01 · Concept

This isn't just a hackathon.

You're not just building a solution. You're building like a startup.

Participants receive real-world problems sourced from NGOs, foundations and partner organisations working with people with disabilities. The goal is not merely to submit code—the goal is to experience the earliest stages of building a startup from zero.

03 · Simulation

Welcome to Day Zero.

Imagine you have an idea but no team.

Imagine your teammates have completely different skills.

Imagine you disagree about what to build.

Imagine you only have a few hours.

Imagine your first idea doesn't work.

Now build anyway.

THE DEFINING TWIST

You don't choose your team.

You won't arrive with your existing friend group and build the project you've already planned. You'll meet new people, discover different skills, divide responsibilities, disagree, adapt, and build something together.

NO PRE-FORMED TEAMS. You must work with the teammates assigned to you. This is a strict rule.

Before AKI HACKS, we built a community.

09 · Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Will swag be provided? +

Of course. 🎁

Will food be provided? +

Of course. 🍱 Food will be provided during the event. There will not be a dedicated lunch break. Eat with your teammates while continuing the experience.

Will there be mentors? +

Yes. There will be more than 3 mentors available to guide participants.

Can I work with my own team? +

No. You must work with randomly assigned teammates. This is a strict rule.

Is AKI HACKS a Japanese hackathon? +

No. 🇮🇳 AKI HACKS is an India-based hackathon. Aki / 秋 means autumn in Japanese. We thought "Autumn Hacks" sounded a little boring, so we chose AKI HACKS.

How can I volunteer? +

Want to help build AKI HACKS from the inside? Volunteer / Core Team Applications forms releasing soon.

Can I become part of the core team? +

We're building the community around AKI HACKS and will open opportunities for volunteers and core-team contributors.
